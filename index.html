Privacy Policy for Machinist Pro

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

MSK Studios built the Machinist Pro app as a Freemium application. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

1. Data Storage & Offline Use

All engineering calculations, hydraulic cylinder designs, QC logs, and project files are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal engineering data on external servers.

2. Third-Party Services (Ads & In-App Purchases)

The free version of the app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, and Google Play Billing to process optional Pro lifetime upgrades. These services may collect device identifiers and purchase history in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
